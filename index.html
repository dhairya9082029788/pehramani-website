Our Story

The Art of Adornment

The brand name Pehramani fundamentally means to adorn. It is rooted deeply in the core belief that adornment is far more than just decoration—it is an essential form of identity, memory, and personal power.

Born in the heart of Mumbai, Pehramani was inspired by vivid childhood memories of a mother’s jewellery box, where every single piece held a unique story or a specific moment in time. We observed a gap between soulful, high-quality craftsmanship and accessible everyday pricing. Pehramani was established to bridge that divide.

We travel directly through the vibrant, bustling fashion lanes of India, collaborating with passionate local artisans to hand-select intention-driven designs. From heavy geometric gold hoops that demand attention to delicate, minimalist sterling bands, our collections are curated for the modern wearer who uses style as a fearless canvas.

We don't just sell jewellery; we curate confidence.
